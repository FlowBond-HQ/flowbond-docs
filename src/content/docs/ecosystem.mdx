<Aside type="caution" title="Non-normative — and the only page where names appear">
Nothing on this page constrains an implementation. It exists because
[Rule 01](/spec/#the-consumer-boundary) keeps product names out of the specification
body, and a reader still reasonably wants to know what has been built.

Listing here is **not** a conformance certification. A conformance claim is a public,
verifiable statement made by the implementer — see [/audit](/audit/).
</Aside>

## How to read this page

The specification does not know its consumers. That is a structural commitment, not
modesty: a Layer 0 whose text names its own applications grows privileged paths for
those applications, and privileged paths are how a protocol quietly becomes a product.

So this page is fenced off. Nothing here may be cited as a normative source, and no
entry on this list has any protocol capability that any other implementer lacks.

## Consumers

Products built on the protocol. A consumer appears here at its own request; the list is
not a ranking, and it is not exhaustive.

*No consumer has yet published a conformance claim against `v0.1-draft`. Entries will be
added as claims are published, each linking to the claim and the tag it was verified
against.*

## Getting listed

1. Publish a conformance claim following [/audit](/audit/): the version, the git tag, the
   primitives implemented, the OPTIONAL behaviours omitted, the proof profiles used, and
   any documented non-conformance.
2. Open an issue on the [specification repository](https://github.com/FlowBond-HQ/flowbond-docs)
   linking to the claim.
3. The editor adds the entry. The editor does **not** verify the claim — the claim is
   yours, published in your name, and a third party can reproduce it.

An entry MUST be removed on request, and MUST be removed if the linked claim disappears.
A listing that outlives its claim is a false endorsement.

## Marks

The FlowBond names and logos are stewarded separately from any software license, by the
Foundation. Listing here does not grant a licence to use them.

You MAY state that your product **implements the FlowBond Protocol `vX.Y`**, and you
SHOULD link to your conformance claim when you do. You MUST NOT describe an
implementation as *certified*, *official* or *endorsed*, because no such status exists.
