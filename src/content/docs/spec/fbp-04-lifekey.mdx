`FBP-04` is the primitive for arrangements that must hold when the parties are not in
the room — and, eventually, when some of them are gone. A **trust** is a sealed set of
rules with named parties, signatories, thresholds and conditions, which changes only by
its own terms.

## Invariants

<Aside type="danger" title="Normative — FBP-04 invariants">
1. **The trust governs its own amendment.** A trust MUST be amendable only through the rules sealed into it at creation. No operator, no platform, no majority outside the sealed rules MAY amend it.
2. **The counselor is NEVER a signatory.** The counselor role is advisory-scoped. It MUST NOT sign, MUST NOT count toward any threshold, and MUST NOT hold a key that can act.
3. **Soulbound bindings are non-transferable.** A binding created by a trust MUST NOT be transferred, sold, delegated or assigned. Recognition MUST NEVER be convertible into equity or into a transferable claim.
4. **Conditions are provable without disclosure.** Whether a condition is met MUST be provable to a verifier who learns nothing else about the trust.
5. **Sealed at creation, appended forever.** The trust's rules, once sealed, MUST NOT be edited. Every subsequent state is an appended event evaluated against the seal.
</Aside>

## §4.1 The trust object

```json title="Trust — normative schema"
{
  "v": 1,
  "id": "tr_01J94…",
  "stream": "s_c31a…",
  "seal": {
    "terms_commitment": { "alg": "sha256", "digest": "…", "salt_commitment": "0x…" },
    "sealed_at": "e_01J94…",
    "amendment_rule": "amd_01J94…"
  },
  "parties": [
    { "party": "fbid:z6Mk…S", "role": "settlor" },
    { "party": "fbid:z6Mk…T1", "role": "trustee" },
    { "party": "fbid:z6Mk…T2", "role": "trustee" },
    { "party": "fbid:z6Mk…B1", "role": "beneficiary" },
    { "party": "fbid:z6Mk…C", "role": "counselor" }
  ],
  "signatories": {
    "members": ["fbid:z6Mk…T1", "fbid:z6Mk…T2", "fbid:z6Mk…S"],
    "threshold": 2,
    "quorum_rules": ["no_single_party_class"]
  },
  "conditions": ["cond_01J94…"],
  "bindings": ["bd_01J94…"],
  "successor_rule": "succ_01J94…",
  "status": "active"
}
```

### Roles

| Role | MAY sign | Counts toward threshold | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `settlor` | MAY | MAY | The party who creates and seals the trust. |
| `trustee` | MUST be able to | MUST | Acts under the sealed rules. At least one is REQUIRED. |
| `beneficiary` | MUST NOT, unless also a trustee | MUST NOT, as beneficiary | Receives under conditions. |
| `counselor` | MUST NOT | MUST NEVER | Advisory-scoped. See §4.3. |
| `observer` | MUST NOT | MUST NOT | Read access under a scoped view key only. |

Normative rules:

- Role assignment MUST be explicit. A party holding two roles MUST be listed twice, and
  the constraints of the stricter role apply.
- `quorum_rules` constrain how a threshold may be met. `no_single_party_class` REQUIRES
  that a satisfying quorum draw from more than one role class, preventing a group of
  co-interested parties from acting alone.
- A trust MUST have at least one trustee and at least one condition. A trust with no
  conditions is a list of names.

## §4.2 Thresholds and signatories

- `threshold` MUST be an integer `≥ 2` where more than one signatory exists, and MUST NOT
  exceed the number of members.
- A signature MUST be an event signed by that party's FBID key. A signature MUST NOT be
  delegable to an agent — a Plane 3 agent MUST NOT ever satisfy a trust threshold, under
  any gate. This is a hard boundary: agents spend, they do not consent.
- Threshold evaluation MUST be deterministic and MUST use only ledger events.
- Where a signatory's FBID is retired, the `successor_rule` governs. If the rule does not
  cover the case, the trust MUST enter `blocked` status rather than proceed with a
  reduced threshold. A silently reduced threshold is the failure mode this primitive
  exists to prevent.

## §4.3 The counselor role

<Aside type="danger" title="Advisory-scoped, and only advisory">
A counselor MAY read what a scoped view key permits, MAY publish advisory events, and
MAY be REQUIRED to be consulted before an action. A counselor MUST NEVER sign, MUST
NEVER count toward a threshold, MUST NEVER hold a key that can act on the trust, and
MUST NEVER be able to block an action that the sealed rules permit.
</Aside>

- A trust MAY require an `fbp04.counsel.recorded` event to exist before a given action.
  Requiring the *existence* of advice is permitted; requiring the advice to be favourable
  is NOT — that would make the counselor a signatory by another name.
- Counsel MUST be recorded as an appended event with a commitment to its content, so
  that it is later provable that advice was given, and provable what it said if the
  parties disclose it.
- A counselor's grant MUST be independently revocable and MUST NOT be a condition of the
  trust's continued operation.

**Rationale (non-normative).** The advisor who gradually becomes a decision-maker is the
oldest failure mode in fiduciary arrangements. Separating "was consulted" from "consented"
at the protocol level makes the drift impossible rather than merely discouraged.

## §4.4 Conditions

Conditions reuse the `FBP-02` condition kinds and add three:

| Kind | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `age_reached` | A beneficiary has reached an age, proven as a predicate over an FBP-01 claim. |
| `milestone_attested` | A named milestone attested by a quorum defined in the seal. |
| `succession_triggered` | A successor rule has fired, e.g. a signatory's retirement or an incapacity attestation. |

- A condition MUST be evaluable from ledger events plus proofs. A condition requiring an
  off-ledger judgement MUST be expressed as an attestation by a named party, so that the
  judgement itself is attributable.
- `age_reached` MUST be satisfied by a zero-knowledge predicate proof. A date of birth
  MUST NOT be transmitted to satisfy it.
- Condition evaluation MUST be monotonic, as in `FBP-02` §2.2.
- The `on_fail` behaviour of a trust condition MUST be `hold`. A trust MUST NOT
  distribute on an unmet condition under any circumstance.

## §4.5 Soulbound bindings

A **binding** attaches something to a party permanently: a recognition, an honour, a
membership, a right that is personal and not economic.

```json title="Binding"
{
  "id": "bd_01J94…",
  "trust": "tr_01J94…",
  "holder": "fbid:z6Mk…B1",
  "kind": "recognition",
  "transferable": false,
  "revocable_by": "sealed_rule",
  "commitment": { "alg": "sha256", "digest": "…", "salt_commitment": "0x…" }
}
```

- `transferable` MUST be `false`. A binding MUST NOT be transferred, sold, lent,
  wrapped, or represented by a transferable token. An implementation MUST NOT expose an
  interface capable of expressing a transfer.
- A binding MUST NOT confer an economic claim. **Recognition is NEVER equity.** Where a
  party is meant to receive value, that is an `FBP-02` split or an `FBP-03` credit — not
  a binding.
- A binding MAY be revoked only where the sealed rules provide for it, and revocation
  MUST be an appended event that leaves the original binding readable as history.
- A binding MUST be presentable as a zero-knowledge proof — "the holder holds a binding
  of this kind from this trust" — without revealing the holder, the trust, or the other
  bindings that trust has issued.

## §4.6 Amendment and succession

<Mermaid title="Amendment path: only the sealed rule can change a sealed trust.">
flowchart TD
  A["Proposed amendment"] --> B{"Does the sealed<br/>amendment_rule permit<br/>this class of change?"}
  B -- no --> R["REJECT · the trust cannot be<br/>amended from outside its own rules"]
  B -- yes --> C{"Quorum met under<br/>signatories + quorum_rules?"}
  C -- no --> H["HOLD · pending signatures"]
  C -- yes --> D{"Counsel required<br/>by the seal?"}
  D -- yes --> E["fbp04.counsel.recorded<br/>MUST exist · content not binding"]
  E --> F
  D -- no --> F["Mandatory delay elapses"]
  F --> G["fbp04.trust.amended appended<br/>original seal remains readable"]
</Mermaid>

- The `amendment_rule` MUST be sealed at creation and MUST itself be amendable only if it
  explicitly says so. A trust whose amendment rule can be replaced by a simple majority
  is not sealed.
- An amendment MUST NOT alter events already applied. Prior distributions stand.
- An amendment MUST carry a mandatory delay expressed in ledger progress, during which
  any party with standing under the seal MAY register an objection event. Objection does
  not block unless the seal says it does; it makes the disagreement part of the record.
- **Succession** MUST be defined at creation: what happens when a signatory dies, is
  incapacitated, retires their FBID, or becomes unreachable. Incapacity MUST be
  established by an attestation quorum named in the seal, never by a platform's
  judgement.

## §4.7 Zero-knowledge obligations

| MUST be provable | MUST NOT be revealed |
| --- | --- |
| A condition is met (age, milestone, succession) | The condition's terms |
| A quorum of signatories signed | Which signatories, to non-parties |
| A holder holds a binding of a given kind | The trust, the corpus, or the other holders |
| A distribution was permitted by the seal | The distribution amount or the corpus |
| The counselor was consulted | What the counsel said |

- The **corpus** — what the trust holds — MUST NOT be public protocol state.
- A beneficiary MUST be able to prove entitlement to a third party (a school, a
  registrar, a counterparty) without disclosing the trust's terms or the identities of
  other beneficiaries.
- Disclosure to a court or regulator MUST proceed through a scoped view key issued under
  the sealed rules, and the issuance MUST be an appended event.

## §4.8 Reference instance (non-normative)

The pattern this primitive was drawn from is a **family trust**: a settlor, two trustees
drawn from different sides of a family, beneficiaries who are minors at sealing time, an
advisor who is deliberately given no vote, and distributions that unlock on ages and
milestones. Nothing in that pattern is privileged by the protocol — it is one shape a
sealed rule set can take, and it is described here only because it makes the roles
concrete.

## §4.9 Conformance checklist

- [ ] No operator, platform or outside majority can amend a sealed trust.
- [ ] The counselor cannot sign, cannot count toward a threshold, and cannot block.
- [ ] An agent cannot satisfy a signature under any gate or delegation.
- [ ] A retired signatory triggers the successor rule or blocks — never a silent
      threshold reduction.
- [ ] Bindings are non-transferable and inexpressible as a transfer in the API.
- [ ] No binding carries an economic claim.
- [ ] `age_reached` is satisfied with no date of birth transmitted.
- [ ] The corpus is not derivable from public state.
